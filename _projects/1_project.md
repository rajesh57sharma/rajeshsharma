## Project Overview

This research project was undertaken under the National
Postdoctoral Fellowship (NPDF) at the Indian Institute of
Science (IISc), Bengaluru, under the supervision of
Prof. Tanmoy Das.

The project focused on investigating the electronic,
magnetic and topological properties of superconducting
systems on square-octagon lattices.

The research explored the interplay between lattice
geometry, electronic correlations, magnetic interactions
and superconducting pairing, with the aim of understanding
the emergence of unconventional superconducting and
topological phases.

## Research Objectives

The major objectives of this project were:

1. Investigating the electronic band structures and
   underlying physical properties of square-octagon lattices.
2. Understanding magnetic interactions and correlated
   electronic phases.
3. Exploring superconducting pairing mechanisms and
   possible unconventional superconducting states.
4. Examining the topological characteristics of the
   electronic and superconducting phases.

## Research Methodology

The project involved theoretical investigations of
electronic and superconducting properties using
appropriate analytical and computational approaches.

Particular attention was given to the role of lattice
geometry, electronic structure, magnetic interactions
and superconducting pairing symmetry.

## Research Outcomes

The research contributed to understanding the interplay
of electronic correlations, magnetism, superconductivity
and topology in square-octagon lattice systems.
