I am Dr. Rajesh Sharma, Assistant Professor in the Department of Science and Humanities at Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Institute of Technology (DBRAIT), Sri Vijaya Puram. 

My academic and research interests lie primarily in theoretical and computational condensed matter physics, particularly the electronic, magnetic, and topological properties of quantum materials.

My research focuses on understanding the interplay of electronic correlations, lattice geometry, magnetism, spin–orbit coupling, and crystal symmetry in low-dimensional quantum materials. I am particularly interested in unconventional electronic and magnetic phases, symmetry-protected electronic structures, and emergent phenomena in two-dimensional materials. My recent research interests also extend to moiré superlattices, flat-band physics, unconventional superconductivity, and correlated phases beyond conventional honeycomb lattices.

I employ theoretical modelling, tight-binding Hamiltonians, and first-principles electronic-structure calculations to investigate the microscopic mechanisms underlying emergent quantum phenomena. I am interested in connecting fundamental theoretical insights with experimentally relevant signatures in novel quantum materials.

Alongside my research activities, I am involved in academic administration, research development, and institutional initiatives to strengthen the research and innovation ecosystem at DBRAIT. I am particularly interested in fostering interdisciplinary research, encouraging student participation in scientific research, and developing collaborative research opportunities.
